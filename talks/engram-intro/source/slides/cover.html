Engram

A knowledge graph that lights up as you learn

[Presenter name] · [Event] · github.com/enspyrco/engram

Engram turns what you read into a knowledge graph, then teaches it back to you with spaced repetition. The graph on the right is the app’s real visual language: each dot is a concept, colour is how well you know it, and the cyan ones are things you just learned settling in among what you already knew. Fifteen minutes: why, how, what you see, and where it’s going.
