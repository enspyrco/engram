A sync peer, not the boss: on launch, every five minutes, and when the app pauses or resumes.

A happy accident: a knowledge graph is naturally merge-friendly. Concepts only get added, a field takes its latest write, and team contributions only add up.

Engram is local-first. Drift, a SQLite layer for Flutter, is the primary store. Sync uses conflict-free replicated data types: a hybrid logical clock stamps each change, the last writer wins per field, and deletions are tombstones so they can’t be resurrected by an old device. It turned out the knowledge graph and even the cooperative game map almost directly onto the textbook CRDTs: grow-only sets, last-writer-wins registers and grow-only counters.
