The plan

Type your answer. Claude marks it.

Question

Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder?

Your typed answer

Light is faster than sound, so it arrives first.

Is that a good answer? Good enough? Compared with what?

So the idea is simple: you type what you’d say, and Claude does the marking. But “mark this answer” is a vague instruction, for a model or a person. This answer is right, but is it complete? To mark it fairly you need to know in advance what a full answer has to contain. That’s what a rubric is, next slide.
