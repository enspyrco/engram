Because Engram has a graph, it can see the shape of what you know, including the holes. It finds four kinds of structural gap. For each one it searches your wiki for a page that would fill it, and Claude predicts which new connections that page would create. Tap once to ingest it. Afterwards Engram compares the predicted edges with what actually appeared, so the recommender is graded too. Then “View in Graph” shows the new nodes glowing as they settle in.
