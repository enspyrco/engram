Step 3 · Scheduling

Where a concept sits decides how well you must know it

Position in the graphTarget recallWhy
Guardian-protected97%A teammate has promised to keep it alive
Hub95%Many other ideas lean on it
Repair mission95%The team is rebuilding it after neglect
Leaf85%Nothing depends on it, so let it space out

A higher target means more frequent reviews. A concept counts as mastered once FSRS expects it to last 21 days, and mastering it unlocks the concepts that depend on it.

FSRS lets you choose a desired retention, the probability you’ll still recall a card when it comes due. Most apps use one number for everything. Engram reads it off the graph: forgetting a hub concept breaks a lot downstream, so it gets reviewed more; a leaf can drift further between reviews. The game layer plugs in here too, through guardians and repair missions.
