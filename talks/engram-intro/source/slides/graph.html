Nodes you already know stay pinned in place. New ones fall in among their neighbours by a physics simulation, so where a concept lands says what it’s close to.

The graph is a custom Flutter painter with a force-directed layout: edges act like springs, nodes repel, and there’s some viscosity so it settles smoothly instead of wobbling. The important design choice is pinning: once you’ve seen your graph, it doesn’t reshuffle. New concepts animate in and find their place, which turns spatial memory into a second memory of the material. Colour is the FSRS state of each concept.
