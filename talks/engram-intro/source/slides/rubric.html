The rubric

A rubric is a marking checklist

Like an exam marker’s sheet: “a mark for saying this, a mark for saying that.”

Claude writes one for every card at the moment it makes the card, alongside the question and answer.

Each line is one thing a full answer must say. It’s called a criterion.

Question

Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder?

Rubric · a full answer says…

1

Light travels much faster than sound

2

The flash and the bang start at the same moment

A rubric turns one vague question, “is this good?”, into a few small, checkable ones: “did they say this? did they say that?” A model is much more reliable at small yes-or-no checks than at an overall impression, and we can test it on each one. For our lightning card there are two things a complete answer needs.
