The safety net

When in doubt, review sooner

Again

A failed marking presses Again

Again can only bring a card back sooner. The worst a broken marker can do is cost you one extra review, never let you forget.

Tested before trusted

It earns the right to schedule

Its marks are compared with answers people marked by hand. Only once it passes may it touch your schedule, and it re-sits the test after any change.

Two mistakes are possible and they aren’t equal. Marking too kindly quietly breaks your schedule. Marking too harshly costs one extra review. So every fallback leans towards Again. And the marker runs in shadow mode today: it marks, we measure how often it agrees with human markers, and it gets no say over your reviews until it clears bars we set in advance. Change the prompt, the model or the rules, and it has to pass again.
