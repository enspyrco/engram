Today, you mark your own answers

Question

Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder?

You answer in your head, flip the card, then press:

Again

Hard

Good

Easy

That button decides when you see the card next. Good pushes it further away; Again brings it back soon.

The catch: we’re kind to ourselves. Every “Good” you didn’t quite earn pushes the next review past the day you’d forgotten it.

This is how every flashcard app works, Engram included. You are the marker. People round up: “I basically knew that.” And each generous mark tells the scheduler the memory is stronger than it is, so the card comes back too late, after it’s gone. The next big piece of Engram is taking the marking off you. We’ll follow this one lightning question all the way through.
