How it works

Four steps, one loop

1 · Read

Your wiki

Point Engram at an Outline wiki collection. Your notes, a course, a team’s docs.

2 · Extract

Claude

Pulls out concepts, how they relate, and quiz questions, each with a predicted difficulty.

3 · Schedule

FSRS

A modern spaced-repetition algorithm decides what you review today.

4 · See

The graph

Every concept is a node, coloured by how well you know it right now.

The interesting part isn’t any one step. It’s that step 2 and step 3 talk to each other.

The pipeline is simple to say. Read from a wiki, have Claude pull out a graph and quiz cards, schedule them with FSRS, and show the result as a living graph. Most tools stop at a pipeline. What makes Engram different is that extraction and scheduling feed each other, which is the next two slides.
