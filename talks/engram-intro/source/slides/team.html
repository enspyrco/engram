The cooperative game

Learning as a team sport

A team shares one graph, a synaptic web. Neglect it and it doesn’t quietly fade. It breaks, and the team rebuilds it together.

Guardians

Volunteer to protect a cluster. Its concepts get a higher recall target.

Goals & glory

Shared targets with tracked contributions, and a leaderboard for who held the line.

Storms & relays

Entropy storms make health drop faster; relay challenges pass work around the team.

Catastrophe

Health falls through four tiers: brownout, cascade, fracture, collapse. Repair missions bring it back.

The social layer rests on the same scheduling. A guardian who protects a cluster raises its desired retention, so the protection is real, not cosmetic. Network health combines mastery, freshness and critical paths, and when it drops the graph visibly degrades through four tiers. The drama comes from watching it break; the satisfaction comes from fixing it together. Friends find each other through a shared wiki, and can challenge or nudge each other.
